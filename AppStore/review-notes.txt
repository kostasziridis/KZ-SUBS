KZ-SUBS creates subtitles from the speech in a video, entirely on the device.

No account, no sign-in and no demo credentials are needed. Every feature is
available immediately after installing.

PLEASE READ FIRST — THE ONE-TIME MODEL DOWNLOAD

The first time you generate subtitles, the app downloads the speech recognition
model it needs: roughly 600 MB, once, over the network. The screen names this
plainly and shows its progress, and the app cannot produce subtitles until it
finishes. On a slow connection this can take several minutes. Please allow it to
complete on Wi-Fi before concluding that the app is not working — this is the only
step that requires a network connection, and nothing else in the app uses one.

The model files are hosted publicly and are downloaded anonymously:
  - huggingface.co/argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml

HOW TO TEST

1. Tap "Choose Video" and pick any video containing speech. Greek and English
   both work; the sample videos in the Simulator have no audio, so please use a
   device video with a voice in it.
2. Choose an accuracy level and allow the one-time download to finish.
3. Subtitles are generated and a style chooser appears. Pick any style.
4. In the editor: tap "Style" for colours, size and position; drag the caption on
   the video to move it and pinch to resize it; tap a subtitle to jump to it, or
   "Edit" to change its words.
5. Tap the share icon to export. "Create video with subtitles" writes a new video
   file; "Create .srt file" writes a subtitle file. Both can be shared or saved.

PRIVACY

No data is collected, in any category. The video and audio are read and processed
on the device and are never uploaded anywhere. There is no account system, no
analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server belonging to us. The App
Privacy answers are "Data Not Collected" throughout, and that is literal.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS

The app asks for add-only photo library permission, and only when the user taps
"Save to Photos" for a finished export. It never reads the photo library: videos
are brought in through the system picker, which hands over only the one file the
user chose. The original video is never modified.
