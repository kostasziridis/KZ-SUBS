## English (Primary)

### App Name (30)
KZ-SUBS

### Subtitle (30)
Offline subtitles for video

### Promotional Text (170)
Subtitles from your own voice, made entirely on your iPhone. Greek and English, word-by-word karaoke styles, and a video your original file never leaves to create.

### Keywords (100)
caption,captions,srt,transcribe,speech,text,karaoke,greek,editor,reels,shorts,burn,words,voice

### Description (4000)
KZ-SUBS turns what you say into subtitles, without sending a single second of your video anywhere.

Choose a video, pick a look, and the words appear — timed to your speech, ready to edit and burn in.

EVERYTHING HAPPENS ON YOUR IPHONE

Your video and audio never leave this device. No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising. After the one-time setup, it works with no internet at all — on a plane, on a boat, in the middle of a field.

GREEK AND ENGLISH, PROPERLY

Built for Greek from the start: correct accents, Greek punctuation, and capitals that drop the tonos the way Greek is actually written. English works just as well, and the app itself speaks whichever language your iPhone does.

SUBTITLES THAT LOOK LIKE YOURS

• Six styles, including word-by-word karaoke and a box that travels with the spoken word
• Any colour you like — the text, the spoken word, the background, or one single word you want to stand out
• Drag to place and pinch to size, straight on the video
• Short bursts of three to six words, the way short-form video reads

FIX IT IN SECONDS

• Tap a word to correct its spelling — its timing stays exactly where it was, so the karaoke still lines up
• Check the spelling of a whole video in one pass, with suggestions for every suspect word
• Teach it names it would otherwise guess at — people, places, brands — so they come out right the first time
• Two speech engines to choose between, because they mishear different things

WHEN IT IS READY

• A new video with the subtitles drawn permanently in. Your original file is never touched.
• Or an .srt file, which works with YouTube, Premiere, VLC and most editors

THE FIRST RUN

The first time you make subtitles, the app downloads the model that understands speech — around 600 MB, once, over Wi-Fi. The screen tells you how long it should take. After that the app never needs the internet again.

## App Privacy questionnaire

Answer **Data Not Collected** for every category. This is literally true: the app has
no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The only network
request it ever makes is downloading the speech model, which sends nothing about the
user.
